# MacroLoom Support

For help with MacroLoom, contact:

**Email:** [macroloom.support@gmail.com](mailto:macroloom.support@gmail.com)

## Common support topics

### Restore a purchase

1. Open MacroLoom.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Select **Unlock Premium**.
4. Select **Restore Purchases**.

Use the same Apple account that made the original purchase.

### Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are administered by Apple:

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
2. Tap your name.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **MacroLoom**.

Deleting the MacroLoom app or account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

### Export your data

1. Open MacroLoom **Settings**.
2. Select **Prepare data export**.
3. Use the share sheet to save or send the generated JSON file.

The export contains the account and profile information held in MacroLoom's backend, meal diary and food items, weight and water entries, subscription status, AI usage and analysis-request records, and temporary download links for retained meal photographs. Photo links expire after one hour, so download any photographs you want to keep promptly.

### Request a complete copy of your personal data

The in-app export is designed to provide your core MacroLoom account and service data quickly. A complete access request may also require a search of records held separately by support, analytics, diagnostics or legally required retention systems.

To request that broader copy, email [macroloom.support@gmail.com](mailto:macroloom.support@gmail.com) with the subject **Personal data access request**. State that you are requesting a copy of your personal data and include the Apple relay email address associated with MacroLoom if you know it. Reasonable information may be requested to verify account ownership before personal data is released. Do not send Apple identity tokens, passwords or payment-card details.

### Delete your account

1. Open MacroLoom **Settings**.
2. Select **Delete account**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

This removes the MacroLoom authentication account and associated active app data, including the profile, diary, weight history and stored meal photographs, subject to the limited legal-retention and backup periods explained in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html).

The App Store subscription must be cancelled separately.

### Report a technical problem

When emailing support, please include:

- A short description of what happened.
- The screen or feature you were using.
- Your iOS version and MacroLoom app version.
- Whether the problem happens repeatedly.
- Any error message shown in the app.

Do not email passwords, Apple identity tokens, payment-card details, private authentication credentials or photographs containing information you do not want included in the support request.

### Privacy or data-rights request

Email [macroloom.support@gmail.com](mailto:macroloom.support@gmail.com) with the subject **Privacy request**. Reasonable information may be requested to verify that you control the relevant account.

The full [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html) explains the available choices and rights.
